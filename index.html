About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at UT Southwestern Medical Center working on multimodal foundational AI models for medicine and healthcare. My research spans knowledge distillation, efficient deep learning, brain foundation models, sample efficient learning, 3D pose estimation, and computer vision. I completed my Ph.D. (in CSE), M.S. (in CSE), M.Sc.(in ICT) and B.Sc. (in ICT) at Kyung Hee University, South Korea, supported by the Korean Presidential Scholarship, and Islamic University, Bangladesh, respectively. I am eager to contribute to cutting-edge AI research by leveraging my expertise in artificial intelligence, deep learning, knowledge distillation, preserving prior knowledge and representation, and efficient model design to address key challenges in healthcare and medicine by developing robust multimodal brain foundation models (BFMs), digital twin brain, computer vision, biometrics, causal learning, biomarker detection, and disease prediction. My goal is to develop innovative, scalable solutions through academic research by leveraging modern state-of-the-art AI technologies to enhance model performance in real-world medical scenarios to diagnose diseases by analyzing MRI, CT scan, ECG, EEG, and other multimodal medical data. My research directly tackles problems such as subject-invariant learning, segmentation under distribution shift, multi-modal action understanding, causal learning, active learning, catastrophic forgetting, task transfer, and computational efficiency.

Tutorials

Hands-on notebooks and guides bridging research concepts with practical implementation. All code is open-source and runs on Google Colab (free GPU tier).

MRI Processing Pipeline — DICOM/NIfTI to Transformer-Ready Tensors 7 notebooks

A complete step-by-step series for neuroscientists transitioning from FSL to Python/PyTorch. Covers the full preprocessing pipeline from raw scanner data to transformer input tokens, with theory and intuition at every step.

01
Load DICOM & NIfTI — Affine Matrix, Metadata, First Tensor
Read scanner files with nibabel, pydicom, and SimpleITK. Understand the affine matrix, DICOM slice sorting, and building the [B, C, D, H, W] tensor.
NotebooknibabelSimpleITKPyTorchColab
Colab ↗GitHub
02
Skull Stripping — HD-BET, ANTsPy, and Heuristic Methods [Cooming soon]
Remove non-brain tissue using three approaches: HD-BET (deep learning), ANTsPy atlas-based, and Otsu+morphology. FSL BET equivalent in Python.
NotebookHD-BETANTsPyBrain MRI
Colab ↗GitHub
03
Bias Field Correction — N4ITK via SimpleITK & ANTsPy [Cooming soon]
Correct MRI intensity inhomogeneity with N4ITK. Understand the multiplicative bias model, B-spline fitting, and how it sharpens tissue histogram peaks.
NotebookSimpleITKANTsPyPreprocessing
Colab ↗GitHub
04
Registration to MNI Space — Rigid, Affine, and SyN (ANTs) [Cooming soon]
Align brains to MNI152 using rigid (6 DOF), affine (12 DOF), and deformable SyN registration. FSL FLIRT/FNIRT equivalent with label map warping.
NotebookANTsPyTorchIOMNI152
Colab ↗GitHub
05
Intensity Normalization — Z-score, Percentile, Histogram Matching [Cooming soon]
Compare five normalization strategies. Learn why statistics must be computed on brain voxels only, and integrate everything with TorchIO DataLoaders.
NotebookTorchIOPyTorchMulti-site
Colab ↗GitHub
06
MRI Augmentation — Spatial, Intensity, and Artifact Simulation [Cooming soon]
MRI-specific augmentation cookbook: valid flips, elastic deformation, bias field simulation, Rician noise, and motion artifacts. Covers the geometric/intensity split for paired label maps.
NotebookTorchIOPyTorchData Aug
Colab ↗GitHub
07
Patchification & 3D Vision Transformer — Volume to Token Sequence [Cooming soon]
Build a 3D ViT from scratch: patch embedding, multi-head self-attention, positional encoding, and CLS-token attention visualization. Full pipeline from NIfTI to classification logits.
NotebookPyTorcheinopsViT · 3D
Colab ↗GitHub
Knowledge Distillation — From Logits to Feature-Level Transfer [Cooming soon] 3 notebooks
